Project Scope

Comprehensive overview of our research approach and problem space.

Literature Survey

Life insurance operations in Sri Lanka remain largely manual and agent-driven, with claims processing, risk assessment, and compliance checks requiring significant human effort. Recent advances in large language models, reinforcement learning, multimodal biometrics, and retrieval-augmented generation present compelling opportunities to automate OPD claims adjudication, adaptive risk scoring, sanctioned entity screening, and personalised policy recommendation at scale.

Research Gap

  • No AI-driven OPD claims automation or intelligent email triage exists for Sri Lankan insurers.
  • Risk scoring relies on static rule-based weights that cannot adapt to individual customer profiles.
  • Sanctioned entity detection lacks multimodal biometric and transliteration-aware name matching.
  • Policy recommendation tools offer no explainability or document-grounded conversational follow-up.
  • Existing platforms fail to identify and close coverage gaps via personalised rider suggestions.

Research Problem

Union Assurance PLC faces operational challenges across the full insurance lifecycle — from slow, manual OPD claims adjudication and reactive email handling, to static risk scoring that ignores individual customer context, inadequate sanctions screening with no biometric layer, and policy recommendations that lack transparency and post-sale conversational support — resulting in processing delays, compliance exposure, and poor customer experience.

Main Objective

Develop a unified AI-powered insurance automation system combining GenAI-driven claims processing and email classification, reinforcement learning-based risk scoring and policy optimisation, multimodal sanctioned entity detection, and an explainable policy recommendation engine with a scope-locked conversational AI agent.

Sub Objectives

  • Automate OPD claims adjudication using OCR, fine-tuned LLMs, and XGBoost confidence scoring.
  • Implement a PPO reinforcement learning agent for adaptive per-customer risk weight adjustment.
  • Build a dual-track face and name sanctions screening pipeline using EfficientNet-B0 and FAISS.
  • Deliver explainable policy recommendations via XGBoost, SHAP, RAG, and a scope-locked chat agent.
  • Integrate all components into a web platform backed by FastAPI, Supabase, and ChromaDB.

Methodology

The system pipeline spans four parallel research components covering data ingestion, AI processing, compliance screening, and personalised recommendation.

01

PDF Upload & OCR Extraction

Customer uploads a medical bill PDF. OCR.space API runs client-side to extract raw text, with PyPDF2 as server-side fallback for text-based documents.

02

LLM Field Extraction

Fine-tuned Llama 3.2-1B with LoRA adapter extracts 13 structured fields from OCR text via HuggingFace Spaces Gradio API.

03

Policy Verification

Six sequential SQLite checks confirm policy existence, active status, OPD coverage limit, payment history, and waiting period compliance.

04

Risk Scoring & Weight Adjustment

Rule-based weighted formula produces a 0–100 risk score. A PPO reinforcement learning agent proposes per-customer weight adjustments reviewed by a risk officer.

05

Policy Optimisation

XGBoost predicts customer expense; SHAP explains top-5 drivers; KMeans segments customers; RAG assigns riders; PPO proposes policy-level optimisation actions.

06

Sanctions Screening

Parallel dual-track pipeline runs TF-IDF + FAISS name matching and EfficientNet-B0 face embedding against a prototype bank, fusing scores for a compliance decision.

07

Policy Suitability Scoring

XGBoost regressor scores every indexed policy against a 31-dimensional user feature vector and ranks top-K policies for recommendation.

08

Explainability & Rider Gap Analysis

SHAP TreeExplainer provides per-feature attribution; a gap analyser identifies coverage shortfalls and scores top-3 riders per recommended policy.

09

RAG Narrative & Conversational Agent

ChromaDB MMR retrieval feeds Groq LLaMA 3.3 70B for policy narratives. A scope-locked chat agent handles post-recommendation Q&A with full Supabase session persistence.

Research Components

Four Core Research Areas

Each component addresses a distinct challenge in intelligent insurance automation.

GenAI

GenAI-Powered Claims Automation

Automates OPD claims processing using OCR.space for document digitisation, a fine-tuned Llama 3.2-1B with LoRA for structured field extraction from medical bills, six-point SQLite policy verification, and XGBoost for ML-based approval confidence scoring — reducing manual adjudication time and improving decision consistency.

RISK

Risk Scoring & Policy Optimisation

Produces adaptive 0–100 customer risk scores using a weighted feature formula. A PPO reinforcement learning agent proposes per-customer weight adjustments validated by a risk officer. XGBoost expense prediction, SHAP explainability, KMeans segmentation, and RAG-based rider assignment drive downstream policy optimisation.

BIO

Sanctioned Entity Detection

Dual-track parallel pipeline screens every applicant for sanctions compliance. Track A uses TF-IDF char n-gram, FAISS ANN search, and a Siamese phonetic model for transliteration-robust name matching. Track B uses EfficientNet-B0 face embeddings with O(1) prototype bank cosine similarity. Scores are fused into a single compliance decision.

XAI

Policy Recommender & Conv. AI Agent

XGBoost scores policy suitability across a 31-feature user vector; SHAP TreeExplainer provides per-feature justification; a rule-based gap analyser suggests top-3 riders. ChromaDB MMR retrieval grounds Groq LLaMA 3.3 70B narratives in actual policy clauses. A scope-locked chat agent handles follow-up Q&A with full Supabase session persistence.
