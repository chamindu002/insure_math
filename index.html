InsureMatch

Intelligent Insurance Policy Recommendation System for Sri Lankan Users

InsureMatch is a final-year research project that leverages artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and machine learning to match Sri Lankan users with the most suitable insurance policies based on their personal profiles, financial capacity, risk preferences, and life events — making insurance more accessible and understandable for everyone.

Project Scope

Comprehensive overview of our research approach and problem space.

Literature Survey

Insurance selection in Sri Lanka remains largely agent-driven and manually intensive. Existing digital platforms lack personalisation and do not consider individual risk profiles, life events, or financial capacity. Recent advances in recommender systems, natural language processing, and explainable AI present strong opportunities to transform how insurance products are discovered, compared, and selected by consumers and financial advisors alike.

Research Gap

  • No AI-powered insurance recommender tailored to the Sri Lankan market.
  • Existing chatbots lack domain-specific insurance knowledge.
  • Policy comparison tools ignore user risk profiles and life context.
  • Limited explainability in existing automated recommendation systems.
  • Lack of multilingual support for Sinhala and Tamil-speaking users.

Research Problem

Sri Lankan insurance consumers face significant challenges in identifying, comparing, and selecting appropriate insurance policies due to the complexity of policy terms, lack of personalised guidance, limited digital tools, and an over-reliance on human intermediaries — resulting in under-insurance, mismatched coverage, and poor financial protection outcomes.

Main Objective

Develop an intelligent, AI-powered insurance policy recommendation system that matches Sri Lankan users to the most suitable life, health, motor, and general insurance products based on personalised profiling, NLP-driven query understanding, and explainable machine learning models.

Sub Objectives

  • Build a user profiling module capturing demographics, risk appetite, and life events.
  • Develop an NLP engine to process natural language insurance queries.
  • Train recommendation models on Sri Lankan insurance product datasets.
  • Implement explainable AI output to justify recommendations.
  • Deliver a responsive, multilingual web interface for end users.

Methodology

The system pipeline covers user profiling, query processing, recommendation, explanation, and feedback.

01

User Onboarding & Profiling

Users register and complete a structured profile including age, income, dependants, occupation, existing coverage, and risk tolerance.

02

NLP Query Understanding

Natural language inputs are parsed using transformer-based NLP models to identify insurance intent, policy type preferences, and coverage requirements.

03

Policy Data Ingestion

Insurance product data from major Sri Lankan insurers is ingested, cleaned, and structured into a standardised knowledge base for downstream matching.

04

Risk Profiling Engine

Machine learning classifiers assess user risk profiles across life, health, and motor insurance dimensions to segment users for targeted recommendations.

05

Recommendation Models

Collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, and hybrid ML models rank and recommend policies aligned with user profiles and stated preferences.

06

Explainability Layer

SHAP and LIME-based explainability modules generate plain-language justifications for each recommendation, improving user trust and transparency.

07

Policy Comparison Module

Users can compare up to four recommended policies side-by-side across premium, coverage, exclusions, and claim settlement ratio dimensions.

08

Feedback & Retraining Loop

User feedback and interaction signals are captured to continuously improve model performance and recommendation relevance over time.

09

Deployment & Integration

The full system is deployed as a cloud-hosted web platform with REST APIs, React frontend, and a Python/Django ML backend.

Research Components

Four Core Research Areas

Each component addresses a distinct challenge in intelligent insurance recommendation.

GenAI

GenAI-Powered Claims Automation

Intelligent communication and claims automation using Generative AI to streamline insurance workflows, reduce processing time, and improve customer experience.

BIO

Sanctioned Entity Detection

Comprehensive sanctioned entity detection using multimodal biometric and identity attribute recognition to prevent fraudulent insurance applications.

RISK

Health Risk & Mortality Predictor

AI-driven personal health risk and mortality prediction engine that informs premium calculation and policy eligibility assessments for life insurance products.

XAI

Policy Recommender & Conv. AI Agent

Explainable, multi-modal policy recommender combined with a conversational AI agent delivering personalised insurance product suggestions with transparent reasoning.
